## 3. Creating a High-Impact Review

Help the community by providing specific, actionable insights.

* **Visual Proof:** Upload up to three high-resolution photos. 
* **Accuracy:** Compare the physical item to the original listing description.
* **Logistics:** Rate the packaging security (PAL) or seller responsiveness (PAP).

<Callout variant="info">
  **UX Tip:** If you encountered an issue, we recommend using **Artemia Chat** to resolve it with the seller before posting a negative review. Most sellers prefer to fix a mistake than receive a low rating.
</Callout>

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I edit my review?
You have a **48-hour window** to edit your review after posting. Once a seller responds or the window closes, the review is locked.

### Can sellers respond?
Yes. Professional sellers often respond to thank you or resolve a listed grievance.

### How do ratings affect visibility?
* **High Ratings:** Boosts search rankings and unlocks the **Top Rated** badge.
* **Low Ratings:** Sellers falling below a specific quality threshold may face restricted listing privileges or account suspension.
