## 2. Fulfillment-Specific Workflows

Return logistics depend on whether the order was fulfilled via <strong>PAL</strong> or <strong>PAP</strong>.

<Tabs>
  <TabsContent label="PAL (Automated)">
    ### Artemia-Managed Returns

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Initiation">
        Buyer submits a return request via <code>Order History</code>.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Claim Review">
        Artemia audits the claim against listing metadata and delivery records.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Return Shipping">
        If approved, a pre-paid <strong>Artemia Return Label</strong> is issued to
        the buyer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verification & Settlement">
        Funds are released once the item is inspected at our fulfillment hub or
        verified by the seller.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </TabsContent>

  <TabsContent label="PAP (Negotiated)">
    ### Seller-Managed Returns

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Direct Resolution Attempt">
        Buyer and seller must attempt a resolution (e.g., replacement or partial
        refund) via <strong>Artemia Chat</strong>.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Return Logistics">
        Seller provides shipping instructions or a custom return label.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Refund Execution">
        Upon receiving the item, the seller manually triggers the refund through
        the dashboard.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Callout variant="warning">
      <strong>Escalation Notice:</strong> If a PAP seller refuses a legitimate
      <em>Not as Described</em> claim, the buyer may escalate directly to a
      <strong>Dispute</strong>.
    </Callout>
  </TabsContent>
</Tabs>

## 3. The Dispute Process

Disputes represent a formal escalation to Artemia’s <strong>Trust & Safety</strong>
team and occur only when direct negotiation fails.

### Investigation Framework

When a dispute is opened, mediators review a standardized
<strong>Evidence Package</strong>:

<ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
  <li>
    <strong>Official Chat Logs:</strong> Only conversations within the Artemia
    platform are considered.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Listing Metadata:</strong> Original listing photos and descriptions
    compared to buyer-submitted evidence.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Fulfillment Records:</strong> Tracking logs and delivery confirmation
    images.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Condition Reports:</strong> High-resolution photos of the item as
    received.
  </li>
</ul>

<Callout variant="danger">
  <strong>Integrity Alert:</strong> Submitting falsified evidence or misleading
  statements violates the Terms of Service and may result in
  <strong>permanent account termination</strong>, regardless of dispute outcome.
</Callout>
