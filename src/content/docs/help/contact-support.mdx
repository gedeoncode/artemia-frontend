## 4. How to Submit an Effective Support Request

Providing complete information upfront dramatically reduces resolution time. Follow this checklist for fastest results.

### The Support Request Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify Your Issue Category">
    Select the most specific category available:
    - Order issues (tracking, delivery, returns)
    - Account access (login, password, verification)
    - Payment problems (declined cards, payout delays)
    - Technical bugs (errors, crashes, missing features)
    - Policy questions (listings, disputes, guidelines)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gather Essential Information">
    **Always include:**
    - **Order ID** (12-digit number from Order Details)
    - **Account email** (the email registered to your account)
    - **Date and time** when the issue occurred
    - **Device and browser** (e.g., "iPhone 14, Safari" or "Windows 11, Chrome")
    
    **For disputes, also include:**
    - Screenshots of the issue
    - Photos of damaged items (external packaging + item itself)
    - Communication history with the other party
    - Tracking numbers and delivery confirmations
  </Step>

  <Step title="Document Your Issue Clearly">
    Write a concise description that includes:
    
    1. **What happened:** "I received a damaged laptop on Jan 5, 2026"
    2. **What you expected:** "The listing showed a brand-new, sealed laptop"
    3. **What you've tried:** "I contacted the seller but received no response"
    4. **What you need:** "I need a full refund and return shipping label"
    
    **Avoid:** Vague descriptions like "It doesn't work" or "Something's wrong"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach Supporting Evidence">
    Upload relevant files directly in your support request:
    
    - **Screenshots** of error messages or unexpected behavior
    - **Photos** showing damage, defects, or discrepancies
    - **Documents** like receipts, invoices, or shipping labels
    - **Videos** demonstrating functional issues (under 2 minutes, under 50MB)
    
    **Accepted formats:** JPG, PNG, PDF, MP4, MOV
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Before Submitting">
    Double-check that you've included:
    - ✓ Clear subject line summarizing the issue
    - ✓ All required identification details
    - ✓ Complete description with specific details
    - ✓ Relevant attachments and evidence
    - ✓ Your preferred resolution outcome
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="warning">
  **Incomplete Requests Delay Resolution:** Support tickets missing order IDs, screenshots, or clear descriptions require follow-up clarification, which can extend resolution time by 24-48 hours.
</Callout>

## 5. What Support Will Never Ask For

Protect yourself from phishing and impersonation attempts.

<Callout variant="danger">
  **Security Guarantee:** Legitimate Artemia support staff will NEVER request:
  
  - Your account password or security questions
  - Two-factor authentication (2FA) codes
  - Complete credit card numbers (we may ask for last 4 digits only)
  - Social Security numbers or government ID numbers via chat/email
  - Wire transfers or cryptocurrency payments for "verification"
  - Remote access to your computer or device
  
  **If someone claiming to be Artemia support asks for any of the above, it's a scam.** Immediately:
  1. Do not provide the information
  2. Screenshot the conversation
  3. Report to safety@artemia.com
  4. Change your password if you've shared any account details
</Callout>

**How to Verify Support Staff:**
- Official support always occurs through artemia.com domains
- Check for the "Verified Staff" badge in live chat
- Support ticket responses come from @artemia.com email addresses
- We'll never ask you to move conversations off-platform

## 7. Dispute Resolution Process

When buyers and sellers cannot resolve an issue directly, Artemia's mediation team steps in.

### How Disputes Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initiate Dispute">
    Navigate to **Order Details → Report a Problem** within 48 hours of delivery. Escrow funds freeze immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Evidence Submission">
    Both parties have 48 hours to submit:
    - Detailed explanations
    - Photos/videos of the item
    - Communication logs
    - Tracking and delivery proof
    - Original listing details
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mediation Review">
    Our disputes team reviews all evidence objectively within 24 hours. We may request additional information from either party.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Resolution Decision">
    Possible outcomes:
    - **Full refund to buyer** (item returned or not as described)
    - **Partial refund** (minor issues, price adjustment)
    - **Favor seller** (buyer's claim not substantiated)
    - **Split resolution** (both parties share responsibility)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Implementation">
    Escrow funds released according to decision. Return labels provided if applicable. Decision is final unless new evidence emerges.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Dispute Outcomes Are Based On:**
- Accuracy of listing description vs. item received
- Quality and clarity of evidence provided
- Platform policies and terms of service
- Communication history between parties
- Seller's track record and buyer's account standing
