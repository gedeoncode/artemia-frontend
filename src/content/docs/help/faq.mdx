## 🌐 General Platform Questions

Understanding Artemia's core features and availability.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="What is the difference between PAP and PAL fulfillment?">
    **PAP (Peer-to-Peer / Seller Fulfilled)**
    - You manage your own shipping and logistics
    - Pay a small upfront listing fee
    - Set your own shipping rates and methods
    - Ideal for local sales, oversized items, or established seller networks
    
    **PAL (Artemia Fulfilled / Professional Logistics)**
    - Free to list—no upfront costs
    - Professional logistics partners handle all shipping
    - Integrated tracking and quality control
    - Commission deducted only after successful sale
    - Best for high-value items requiring standardized handling
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Is Artemia available in my country?">
    Artemia is expanding globally with new regions added regularly. To check if your location is supported for buying or selling:
    
    1. Navigate to **Account Settings → Localization**
    2. View supported countries and shipping zones
    3. Enable notifications for when new regions launch
    
    Currently supported regions include [list major markets]. Check our [Expansion Roadmap](#) for upcoming availability.
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="How does the escrow payment system work?">
    When you make a purchase, your payment is held securely by Artemia—not transferred to the seller. Funds are only released after:
    
    - You confirm receipt and satisfaction with your order, OR
    - The automatic release period (24-48 hours after delivery) expires with no issues reported
    
    This protects both buyers and sellers throughout every transaction.
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="What fees does Artemia charge?">
    **For Buyers:** No fees—you pay only the item price plus shipping and applicable taxes.
    
    **For Sellers:**
    - PAP listings: Small upfront listing fee (varies by category)
    - PAL listings: Commission-based (percentage of final sale price, deducted at payout)
    - No hidden fees or monthly subscriptions
    
    View detailed fee structures in **Seller Dashboard → Pricing & Fees**.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## 🛍️ Buyer Questions

Navigate purchases, tracking, and returns with confidence.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="How do I track my order?">
    **PAL Orders (Artemia Fulfilled)**
    - Real-time tracking integrated directly into **My Purchases → Order Details**
    - Automatic status updates via email and push notifications
    - Direct link to carrier's tracking page
    
    **PAP Orders (Seller Fulfilled)**
    - Sellers must provide valid tracking information once shipped
    - Access tracking links through your order details page
    - If no tracking is provided within 48 hours of shipping confirmation, contact support
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Can I cancel my order?">
    **Before Shipment:** Yes, cancellations are permitted anytime before the order status changes to "Shipped." Full refund issued within 3-5 business days.
    
    **After Shipment:** Cancellation is no longer available. You must wait for delivery and initiate a standard return request if needed.
    
    **Exception:** PAL orders already with the courier cannot be recalled mid-transit due to logistics partner policies.
    
    *To request cancellation: Navigate to **My Purchases → [Order] → Request Cancellation***
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="What if my item arrives damaged or not as described?">
    <Callout variant="danger">
      **Act Immediately:** You must report issues *before* confirming receipt or before the auto-release window expires.
    </Callout>
    
    **Steps to take:**
    1. Do NOT confirm receipt of the item
    2. Navigate to **Order Details → Report a Problem**
    3. Select issue type: "Damaged," "Not as Described," or "Wrong Item"
    4. Upload clear photos showing:
       - External packaging condition
       - Damaged areas of the item
       - Any discrepancies from the listing
    5. Submit your dispute—escrow funds will be frozen immediately
    
    **What happens next:**
    - Our dispute resolution team reviews within 24 hours
    - Seller has 48 hours to respond with their evidence
    - Resolution typically includes full refund, partial refund, or replacement
    - Return shipping labels provided for approved returns
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="How long do I have to inspect my purchase?">
    You have **24-48 hours** after delivery (grace period varies by item category) to:
    - Thoroughly inspect the item
    - Verify it matches the listing description
    - Test functionality for electronics or appliances
    - Report any issues through the dispute system
    
    If no action is taken within this window, funds automatically release to the seller and the transaction is considered complete.
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Can I message sellers directly?">
    Yes! Use the integrated messaging system to:
    - Ask pre-purchase questions about specs, condition, or compatibility
    - Coordinate local pickup for PAP items
    - Request additional photos or information
    
    **Important:** All communication must remain on-platform. Never share personal contact details, bank information, or complete payments outside Artemia.
    
    *Access messaging through the product page or **My Purchases → Contact Seller***
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## 💼 Seller Questions

Optimize your shop, manage inventory, and understand payouts.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="When will I receive payment for my sales?">
    **PAL Sales:** Funds transfer to your Artemia Wallet **24 hours** after the buyer confirms receipt (or after the auto-release period expires), provided no disputes are active.
    
    **PAP Sales:** Same 24-hour timeline after confirmation/auto-release.
    
    **Withdrawal Options:**
    - Bank transfer: 3-5 business days
    - Digital wallet: Instant to 24 hours
    - Minimum withdrawal amount: [specify amount]
    
    *View detailed transaction history in **Seller Dashboard → Payouts***
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="What happens if a buyer doesn't pick up their PAL package?">
    If a PAL package goes unclaimed at the delivery location:
    
    1. Courier attempts delivery 2-3 times over 5-7 days
    2. Package held at local facility for pickup (7-14 days depending on carrier)
    3. If still unclaimed, package returns to your registered return address
    4. Buyer receives automatic refund (minus actual logistics costs incurred)
    5. Your inventory count restores automatically
    6. You may be charged a restocking/return shipping fee
    
    **Prevention tip:** Enable "Signature Required" for high-value items to ensure successful delivery.
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Can I change my shop name or settings?">
    **Shop Name:** Update once every **90 days** via **Seller Dashboard → Shop Settings**
    
    **Other Settings You Can Modify Anytime:**
    - Shop description and banner
    - Business hours and response time
    - Return policies (must meet minimum platform standards)
    - Payment methods and payout preferences
    - Shipping zones and rates (PAP only)
    
    *Note: Frequent shop name changes may affect your seller reputation score.*
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="How do I manage inventory across PAP and PAL?">
    Inventory is automatically synchronized across both fulfillment modes:
    
    - List once, choose fulfillment type per listing
    - Real-time stock updates prevent overselling
    - Convert existing PAP listings to PAL (if eligible)
    - Bulk import/export via CSV for large catalogs
    
    Access inventory management through **Seller Dashboard → My Inventory**.
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="What are the requirements for PAL eligibility?">
    To qualify for Artemia's professional fulfillment services, items must meet:
    
    **Product Requirements:**
    - New or certified refurbished condition
    - Properly packaged and labeled
    - Meet category-specific standards (electronics, fragile items, etc.)
    
    **Seller Requirements:**
    - Verified seller account
    - Minimum seller rating (3.5+ stars for new PAL sellers)
    - Compliance with all platform policies
    
    **Category Restrictions:** Some categories (vehicles, real estate, custom-made items) are PAP-only.
    
    *Check PAL eligibility when creating listings—the system will notify you if your item qualifies.*
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## 🔒 Account & Security

Protect your account and resolve access issues.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="I forgot my password—how do I reset it?">
    1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login screen
    2. Enter your registered email address
    3. Check your inbox for a secure reset link
    4. Create a new strong password (minimum 12 characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
    
    **Security notes:**
    - Reset links expire after 30 minutes for your protection
    - Never share reset links with anyone
    - If you don't receive the email, check spam/junk folders
    - Still having issues? Contact support with your username (never share passwords)
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?">
    Add an extra layer of security to your account:
    
    1. Navigate to **Account Settings → Security**
    2. Select **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**
    3. Choose your preferred method:
       - Authenticator app (recommended: Google Authenticator, Authy)
       - SMS text message
       - Email verification
    4. Follow setup instructions and save backup codes
    
    *Once enabled, you'll need both your password and 2FA code to log in.*
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="Why can't I access certain marketplace features?">
    Feature access depends on:
    
    **Account Verification Status:**
    - Unverified: Basic browsing only
    - Email verified: Full buying access
    - Identity verified: Selling access + higher transaction limits
    
    **Geographic Restrictions:** Some features may be limited based on your location
    
    **Account Standing:** Policy violations may temporarily restrict certain features
    
    Check **Account Settings → Verification Status** to see what's needed to unlock full access.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## 🛠️ Technical & Listing Issues

Troubleshoot common platform and listing challenges.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem title="Why is my listing showing as 'Pending Review'?">
    To maintain marketplace integrity and user safety, our Trust & Safety team manually reviews listings from:
    
    **Always Reviewed:**
    - New seller accounts (first 5 listings)
    - High-value categories (Real Estate, Vehicles, Luxury Goods, Electronics over $1,000)
    - Listings flagged by automated systems for policy concerns
    
    **Review Timeline:**
    - Standard review: 24-48 hours
    - High-value items: Up to 72 hours
    - Expedited review: Available for verified sellers
    
    **Speed up approval:**
    - Complete identity verification
    - Provide detailed, accurate descriptions
    - Upload high-quality photos
    - Include all required documentation
    
    *Track review status in **Seller Dashboard → My Listings***
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="My listing was rejected—what should I do?">
    Review the rejection reason provided via email and in your dashboard. Common causes:
    
    - Prohibited items (weapons, counterfeit goods, restricted substances)
    - Insufficient product details or poor-quality images
    - Pricing errors or misleading descriptions
    - Missing required documentation
    - Policy violations
    
    **To resolve:**
    1. Read the specific rejection reason carefully
    2. Edit your listing to address all concerns
    3. Resubmit for review
    4. Contact support if you believe the rejection was in error
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="I'm experiencing technical issues with the platform">
    **Quick troubleshooting steps:**
    
    1. **Clear cache and cookies:** Often resolves display/loading issues
    2. **Try a different browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
    3. **Disable browser extensions:** Ad blockers can interfere with functionality
    4. **Check your internet connection:** Ensure stable connectivity
    5. **Update your app:** Mobile users should use the latest version
    
    **Still not working?** Report the issue with:
    - Description of the problem
    - Screenshots or screen recording
    - Device/browser information
    - Steps to reproduce the issue
    
    Contact support through **Help Center → Report Technical Issue**
  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem title="How do I report suspicious activity or scams?">
    <Callout variant="danger">
      **Report immediately if you encounter:**
      - Requests for off-platform payment
      - Sellers asking for personal banking details
      - Counterfeit or stolen goods
      - Phishing attempts or impersonation
      - Harassment or threatening behavior
    </Callout>
    
    **To report:**
    - On listings: Click **"⋯" menu → Report Listing**
    - On messages: Click **"Report" button** in conversation
    - On profiles: Visit seller profile → **Report User**
    - General concerns: **Help Center → Report Safety Issue**
    
    Our team investigates all reports within 24 hours. Serious violations may result in immediate account suspension.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
