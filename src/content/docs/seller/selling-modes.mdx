## When Each Mode Applies

<Grid cols={2} gap={4}>
  <Card title="PAP – Pay at Publication" icon="Package">
    PAP is intended for products that require upfront validation due to their
    value, size, or delivery complexity. Sellers publish their listings by paying
    a publication fee before the product is reviewed and made available.
  </Card>

  <Card title="PAL – Pay at Delivery" icon="Truck">
    PAL is designed for standardized products that fit Artemia’s logistics
    network. Listings are verified first and go live without upfront payment,
    with platform commissions applied after delivery.
  </Card>
</Grid>

## Decision Guide

<Tabs>
  <TabsContent label="PAP is a good fit if…">
    <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
      <li>You sell vehicles, real estate, services, or bulky items.</li>
      <li>Your products require custom delivery or negotiation.</li>
      <li>You prefer paying a fixed publication fee instead of commissions.</li>
    </ul>
  </TabsContent>

  <TabsContent label="PAL is a good fit if…">
    <ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
      <li>You sell standard consumer or retail products.</li>
      <li>You want Artemia to manage shipping and delivery.</li>
      <li>You prefer commission-based pricing after successful sales.</li>
    </ul>
  </TabsContent>
</Tabs>

## Important to Know

<Callout variant="info">
  Selling mode selection happens during seller onboarding and product creation.
  If a product category does not match the currently selected mode, Artemia will
  guide you to switch to the appropriate selling mode before publication.
</Callout>
