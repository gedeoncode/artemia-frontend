## 2. Professional Storefront Setup

Use your shop profile to proactively answer buyer questions and reduce
pre-purchase friction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The Store Bio">
    Clearly describe your expertise and specialization (e.g.,
    <em>Certified Pre-Owned Electronics</em>) and highlight your commitment to
    quality fulfillment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Operating Hours">
    Especially important for <strong>PAP sellers</strong>. Define availability
    for chat inquiries, service appointments, or local pickup to set buyer
    expectations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Featured Listings">
    Use the <em>Feature on Profile</em> toggle in your Listing Manager to pin
    priority or seasonal items at the top of your storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Optimization Pro Tips

Apply these best practices to maximize conversion and long-term shop performance.

<ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
  <li>
    <strong>Mobile-First Design:</strong> The majority of Artemia traffic is
    mobile. Preview your logo and banner on smaller screens to avoid text
    cropping.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Response Metrics:</strong> Average response time is publicly visible.
    Faster replies improve your Engagement Score and search placement.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Asset Quality:</strong> Avoid stock imagery for branding. Original,
    high-contrast visuals consistently outperform generic graphics.
  </li>
</ul>
