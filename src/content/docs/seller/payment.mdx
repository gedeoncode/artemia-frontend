# Payments on Artemia (Seller Guide)

<div className="text-xl text-slate-600">
  Artemia supports different payment flows depending on the selling mode and
  transaction type. This page explains how payments work across the platform and
  what is — and is not — handled online.
</div>
<Callout variant="info" inline>
  For the legally binding framework governing transactions, see the Terms of Sale.
  This page provides operational guidance only.
</Callout>

---

## Buyer–Seller Payments

<Callout variant="warning" inline>
  Artemia does not process online payments between buyers and sellers.
</Callout>

All commercial transactions between buyers and sellers on Artemia are handled
**in cash only**.

This applies to:
- Product purchases
- Service payments
- Deliveries and handovers
- Any direct settlement between buyer and seller

Artemia does not act as a payment intermediary for sales and does not hold or
transfer buyer funds.

---

## Online Payments on Artemia

<Callout variant="info" inline>
  Online payments are used exclusively for PAP publication fees.
</Callout>

Artemia supports online payments **only** when a seller is required to pay a
publication fee under the **PAP (Pay at Publication)** selling mode.

Accepted payment providers include:
- Stripe
- PayPal

These payments are used solely to:
- Pay listing publication fees
- Activate PAP product listings

They are **not** used for:
- Buyer payments
- Seller payouts
- Order settlements
- Escrow or fund holding

## Seller Payouts

Artemia does not issue payouts to sellers.

- Sellers receive payments directly from buyers in cash
- No bank transfers, wallets, or online withdrawals are supported
- Artemia does not store seller payment details

Any platform fees or commissions are handled separately from buyer payments and
do not affect cash transactions between buyers and sellers.

---

## Important Notes

<Callout variant="danger" inline>
  Any attempt to process buyer payments online through Artemia is not supported.
</Callout>

- Do not request buyers to pay through Stripe, PayPal, or other online methods
- Do not expect in-app payment confirmation for sales
- Payment terms should be clearly communicated between buyer and seller

Failure to follow these rules may result in listing removal or account action.
