## 2. The Payout Lifecycle (PAL)

To maintain marketplace integrity, PAL funds follow a milestone-based release
schedule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Escrow Secure">
    Buyer funds are collected at checkout and held in a secure,
    non-interest-bearing escrow account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery Milestone">
    Logistics confirms successful delivery. A <strong>24-hour verification
    window</strong> begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wallet Settlement">
    If no dispute is raised, funds (minus commission) are released to your
    <strong>Artemia Wallet</strong>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bank Withdrawal">
    Initiate a payout to your linked bank account. Standard transfers arrive
    within 1–3 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Financial Setup & Compliance

Artemia complies with global AML (Anti-Money Laundering) and KYB
(<em>Know Your Business</em>) regulations.

### Linking Your Bank Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account Details">
    Enter your IBAN/SWIFT or local bank information via
    <code>Finances &gt; Payout Settings</code>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verification">
    Complete micro-deposit confirmation or identity verification as requested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identity Match">
    The bank account holder name must <strong>exactly match</strong> your
    registered Artemia Seller Identity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="danger">
  <strong>Security Audit:</strong> Mismatched or suspicious bank details trigger
  an automatic 48-hour account hold to prevent unauthorized fund redirection.
</Callout>

## 5. Payouts for PAP Listings

Payout handling differs for high-value PAP transactions.

<ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
  <li>
    <strong>On-Platform Checkout:</strong> Funds are released once both buyer and
    seller mark the order as <em>Completed</em>.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Direct Sales:</strong> If finalized off-platform, Artemia’s fee covers
    listing exposure and lead generation only. Capital transfer is handled
    externally.
  </li>
</ul>
