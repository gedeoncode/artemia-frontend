# PAP & PAL — Internal Support FAQ

This document helps Artemia support teams understand how selling modes work,
why certain user flows occur, and how to respond to common seller questions.

---

## What are PAP and PAL?

**PAP (Pay at Publication)** and **PAL (Pay at Delivery)** are the two selling
modes available on Artemia. Each mode determines:
- when a seller pays fees or commissions
- how products are verified
- how logistics and delivery are handled

---

## When does a seller choose a selling mode?

A selling mode is selected during **seller onboarding**.

This choice is required to complete account setup. However, the selected mode
can later be adjusted during product creation if the product category requires
a different mode.

---

## Why is a seller asked to switch selling mode?

A seller is prompted to switch selling mode when:

- their current selling mode does not match the product category
- the category enforces a specific fulfillment or payment flow

This is expected behavior and is not an error.

<Callout variant="info" inline>
  The platform guides the seller through the switch to ensure compliance with
  category rules and operational constraints.
</Callout>

---

## Why is a seller redirected to payment before publication?

This occurs **only for PAP listings**.

In PAP:
1. The seller enters the product price
2. Publication fees are calculated based on price and category
3. The seller is redirected to the payment step
4. The product is submitted for verification after payment

If the seller has not completed payment, the product will not be reviewed or
published.

---

## Why is there no payment step for PAL listings?

PAL listings do not require upfront payment.

In PAL:
- listings are submitted for verification first
- products go live if they meet platform requirements
- commissions are applied after successful delivery

---

## When does a product go live?

- **PAL**: after verification is completed and approved  
- **PAP**: after publication payment is completed **and** verification is approved

If a product is pending, verify whether:
- payment is missing (PAP)
- verification is still in progress

---

## Why does a seller see different fees for different products?

Fees depend on:
- the selected selling mode (PAP or PAL)
- the product category
- the product price

This explains why:
- some products require a publication fee
- others only incur commission after delivery

---

## Does PAP mean Artemia does not verify the product?

No.

All products on Artemia go through verification.
The difference is **when** verification occurs in the flow and **whether payment
is required beforehand**.

---

## Is the selling mode switch automatic?

No.

The platform **proposes** the correct selling mode when needed.
The seller must confirm the switch before continuing.

## Common Support Responses (Quick Copy)

**“Why am I being asked to pay before my product is reviewed?”**  
→ Your product is using the PAP selling mode, which requires a publication fee
before verification.

**“Why can’t I publish this product with my current mode?”**  
→ This product category requires a different selling mode. The platform will
guide you through switching to the correct one.

**“I published my product but it’s not live yet.”**  
→ Check whether payment (PAP) or verification is still pending.
