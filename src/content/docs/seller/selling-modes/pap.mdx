
# PAP – Pay at Publication

<div className="text-xl text-slate-600">
  PAP (Pay at Publication) is a selling mode designed for products that require
  upfront validation due to their value, size, or operational complexity. This
  mode applies a fixed publication fee and gives sellers full control over
  logistics and customer coordination.
</div>

---

## What PAP Is Designed For

PAP is intended for listings where pricing, delivery, or fulfillment cannot be
standardized across transactions.

Typical PAP categories include:

- Vehicles (cars, motorcycles, trucks)
- Real estate and land
- Services
- Heavy, bulky, or custom-built products
- High-value items requiring negotiation or inspection

These products often involve:
- Custom delivery terms
- Offline coordination
- Variable timelines
- Seller-managed logistics

---

## How PAP Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Product creation">
    The seller creates a product listing and selects a category that supports
    PAP. During this step, the product price is entered.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publication fee calculation">
    The platform automatically calculates the publication fee based on:
    <ul className="list-disc pl-5 mt-2 space-y-1">
      <li>The product category</li>
      <li>The listed price</li>
    </ul>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Payment before verification">
    Before the product can be reviewed, the seller is redirected to the payment
    step to complete the publication fee.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Product verification">
    Once payment is completed, the product is submitted for review by the
    Artemia verification team to ensure it meets platform requirements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Product goes live">
    If the listing is approved, it is published and becomes visible to buyers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing & Fees

<Grid cols={2} gap={4}>
  <Card title="Publication Fee">
    PAP listings require an upfront publication fee. This fee is calculated
    automatically during product creation and must be paid before the product
    is reviewed or published.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Platform Commission">
    PAP listings do not incur any sales commission. The seller keeps 100% of the
    sale amount, excluding external service or logistics costs.
  </Card>
</Grid>

---

## Logistics & Fulfillment

Under PAP, all logistics responsibilities remain with the seller.

This includes:
- Delivery coordination
- Shipping or transport arrangements
- Handover scheduling
- Buyer communication related to fulfillment

Artemia does not manage shipping, tracking, or delivery for PAP listings.

---

## Mode Compatibility & Switching

<Callout variant="warning" inline>
  PAP availability depends on the product category.
</Callout>

If a seller initially selects a different selling mode but attempts to publish
a product in a PAP-supported category:

- Artemia will prompt the seller to switch to PAP
- The switch is required to proceed with publication
- Fees and verification rules update automatically

This ensures that each product follows the correct operational flow.

---

## Verification Rules

All PAP products are reviewed by Artemia before going live.

Verification ensures that:
- The product category is correct
- The listing content is accurate and compliant
- Pricing and product information meet platform standards

Payment of the publication fee does not guarantee approval.

---

## When to Use PAP

PAP is recommended if:

- Your products require flexible delivery or negotiation
- You manage your own logistics or third-party freight
- You prefer a one-time publication fee instead of commissions
- Your listings involve high-value or non-standard items
