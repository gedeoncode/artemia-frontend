# PAL – Pay at Delivery

<div className="text-xl text-slate-600">
  PAL (Pay at Delivery) is a selling mode designed for standardized products that
  fit Artemia’s logistics and fulfillment network. This mode allows sellers to
  publish listings without upfront fees, while Artemia manages delivery and
  applies a commission after successful fulfillment.
</div>

---

## What PAL Is Designed For

PAL is intended for products that can be shipped, tracked, and delivered through
a standardized fulfillment process.

Typical PAL categories include:

- Consumer electronics
- Fashion and apparel
- Home and lifestyle products
- Packaged goods
- Other standard retail items

These products generally:
- Have predictable dimensions and weights
- Use fixed pricing
- Follow standardized delivery timelines

---

## How PAL Works

<Steps>
  
  <Step title="Product creation">
    The seller creates a product listing and selects a category that supports
    PAL. Product details and pricing are entered during this step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Product verification">
    Before publication, the product is reviewed by the Artemia verification team
    to ensure it meets platform and logistics requirements.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Product goes live">
    Once approved, the product is published and becomes available to buyers
    without any upfront payment from the seller.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Order fulfillment">
    When an order is placed, Artemia manages shipping, tracking, and delivery
    through its logistics partners.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Payout after delivery">
    After successful delivery, the seller receives their payout automatically,
    minus the applicable platform commission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Pricing & Fees

<Grid cols={2} gap={4}>
  <Card title="Listing Cost">
    PAL listings are free to publish. Sellers are not charged any upfront fees
    during product creation or publication.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Platform Commission">
    A category-based commission is applied after successful delivery. Commission
    rates vary depending on the product category and are calculated
    automatically.
  </Card>
</Grid>

---

## Logistics & Fulfillment

Under PAL, Artemia handles end-to-end logistics.

This includes:
- Shipping coordination
- Order tracking
- Delivery management
- Status updates to buyers and sellers

Sellers are responsible for ensuring inventory availability and accurate product
information.

---

## Mode Compatibility & Switching

<Callout variant="warning">
  PAL availability depends on the product category.
</Callout>

If a seller initially selects a different selling mode but attempts to publish
a product in a PAL-supported category:

- Artemia will prompt the seller to switch to PAL
- The switch is required to proceed with publication
- Commission and logistics rules update automatically

This ensures that each product follows the correct fulfillment flow.

---

## Verification Rules

All PAL products are verified before publication.

Verification ensures that:
- The product can be shipped through Artemia’s logistics network
- Listing details are accurate and complete
- Category selection aligns with delivery requirements

Only approved products are allowed to go live.

---

## When to Use PAL

PAL is recommended if:

- You sell standard consumer or retail products
- You want Artemia to manage shipping and delivery
- You prefer commission-based pricing over upfront fees
- You want a scalable, hands-off fulfillment workflow
