## Addresses & Localization

Artemia’s logistics engine uses your location to calculate real-time shipping for **PAL** orders and filter nearby **PAP** listings.

### Logistics Management
* **Primary Shipping Address:** Your default destination. Ensure building numbers and landmarks are precise to minimize delivery delays.
* **Address Book:** Save multiple locations (Home, Office, Warehouse). 
* **Localization Logic:** Your "Primary" toggle dictates the regional taxes and shipping rates displayed in your cart.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Changing your primary location may update your cart totals in real-time due to varying regional shipping rates.
</Callout>

## Account Lifecycle

If you need to step away from the marketplace, we offer two paths for closing your account.

### Deactivation vs. Deletion
* **Deactivation:** Temporarily hides your profile and listings. Your data remains on our secure servers if you choose to return.
* **Permanent Deletion:** After a **30-day** deactivation grace period, all data is purged from our active databases.

<Callout variant="danger">
  **Critical Checklist:** Before requesting deletion, ensure all **PAL payouts** are processed and all active orders are marked as **Completed**.
</Callout>
