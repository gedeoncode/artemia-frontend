# Security Best Practices
<div className="text-xl text-slate-600">Security at Artemia is a shared responsibility. While our infrastructure is bank-grade, your individual settings are the first line of defense for your funds and data.</div>

---

## 1. Hardening Your Access
Your credentials are the keys to your store and wallet. Ensure they are enterprise-hardened.

<Grid cols={2} gap={6}>
  <Card title="Multi-Factor Authentication" icon="ShieldCheck">
    **Status: Highly Recommended**
    Use TOTP apps (Google/Microsoft Authenticator) instead of SMS to prevent SIM-swapping attacks.
    <br/>
    `Settings > Security > 2FA`
  </Card>
  <Card title="Password Hygiene" icon="Key">
    **Status: Mandatory**
    Use a unique, high-entropy password (12+ characters). Never reuse passwords from your primary email or banking apps.
  </Card>
</Grid>

---

## 2. Transaction Integrity
To remain protected by Artemia’s **Buyer & Seller Protection**, follow the "On-Platform Only" rule.

<Callout variant="danger">
  ### The "Golden Rule" of Marketplace Safety
  **Never pay via external wire transfers, direct bank deposits, or third-party apps (WhatsApp Pay, external crypto wallets).** Off-platform transactions bypass our escrow and dispute resolution systems. If a user asks to "save on fees" by moving off-app, **report them immediately.**
</Callout>

---

## 3. Communication & Phishing Defense
Scammers often attempt to impersonate Artemia staff to harvest 2FA codes or passwords.

### Official Communication Channels
* **Email Domain:** Always verify the sender is `@artemia.com`.
* **The URL:** Ensure your browser shows `https://artemia.com/...` before entering credentials.
* **In-App Chat:** Use the Artemia Chat system for all negotiations. This creates a cryptographically verifiable record for dispute resolution.

<Callout variant="info">
  **Pro-Tip:** Artemia staff will **never** ask for your password or 2FA code via phone, email, or chat. 
</Callout>

---

## 4. Privacy & Device Management
Protect your physical and digital environment while trading.

* **Public Profiles:** Audit your "Shop Bio" to ensure you aren't inadvertently sharing your personal phone number or home address.
* **Session Control:** If using a shared device, always logout and use a "Private/Incognito" window to prevent credential caching.
* **Device Health:** Keep your OS and browser updated to the latest security patch to defend against zero-day exploits.

---

## 5. Incident Response
**What to do if you suspect a compromise:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Immediate Lockout">
    Change your password and navigate to **Security Settings > Active Sessions** to "Revoke All Sessions." This forces all other devices to log out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report Breach">
    Click the **"Report Security Issue"** button in the Help Center. This alerts our SOC (Security Operations Center) to monitor your account for fraudulent payouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Financial Protection">
    If payment methods were linked, notify your bank or card issuer immediately to freeze the associated credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>
