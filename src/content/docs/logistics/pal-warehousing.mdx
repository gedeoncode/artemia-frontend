## 2. The Inbound Process

Follow our standardized "Inbound Workflow" to ensure your inventory is processed and live within 24–48 hours of arrival.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Inbound Request">
    Navigate to <code>Seller Dashboard &gt; Inventory &gt; Send to Warehouse</code> to announce your shipment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Standardized Labeling">
    Affix an Artemia-generated SKU or barcode to every unit. This is critical for automated sorting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transit to Hub">
    Ship your bulk inventory to the assigned Fulfillment Center. Tracking must be provided in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audit & Receipt">
    Our team verifies quantity and quality. Items not matching descriptions are flagged for return.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="warning">
  **Rejection Policy:** Units arriving damaged or improperly labeled will be returned at the seller’s expense to maintain marketplace quality.
</Callout>

## 4. Intelligent Inventory Management

Our backend synchronizes your physical stock with your digital storefront in real-time.

<ul className="list-disc pl-5 space-y-1">
  <li><strong>Real-time Sync:</strong> When a warehouse item is sold, your public "Available Quantity" updates instantly.</li>
  <li><strong>Smart Alerts:</strong> Set custom thresholds to receive "Low Stock" notifications before you lose sales momentum.</li>
  <li><strong>Auto-Sold Out:</strong> If stock hits zero, the listing is paused until a new inbound shipment is verified.</li>
</ul>

## 5. Returns Handling (The Reverse Loop)

When a buyer returns a PAL item, our Quality Control (QC) team handles the inspection so you don’t have to.

<Grid cols={2} gap={4}>
  <Card title="Restockable" icon="CheckCircle">
    Item passes inspection. It is immediately added back to your active inventory for resale.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unsellable" icon="XCircle">
    Item is damaged or used. We move it to "Unsellable" status and await your instruction (Dispose or Return).
  </Card>
</Grid>

<Callout variant="info">
  **Dashboard Visibility:** You can view high-resolution photos of returned "Unsellable" items directly in your Seller Dashboard under <code>Logistics &gt; QC Reports</code>.
</Callout>
