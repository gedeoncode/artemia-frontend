
# Shipping & Logistics Guide
<div className="text-xl text-slate-600">Artemia bridges the gap between buyers and sellers with a multi-modal logistics network. Whether managed by our partners or the seller, here is how to ensure a successful delivery.</div>

---

## 1. Fulfillment Pathways
The logistics experience depends entirely on the **Selling Mode** selected at the time of listing.



<Grid cols={2} gap={6}>
  <Card title="PAL (Artemia Managed)" icon="Truck">
    <Badge variant="primary">Integrated</Badge>
    Best for standard consumer goods. Labels, tracking, and insurance are handled within the app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PAP (Seller Managed)" icon="Package">
    <Badge variant="secondary">Independent</Badge>
    Used for bulky items (vehicles, furniture) or specialized freight where the seller chooses the carrier.
  </Card>
</Grid>

---

## 2. Operating in PAL Mode
In PAL mode, the Artemia logistics engine automates the "Last Mile" experience.

<Tabs>
  <TabsContent label="For Sellers">
    ### Preparing Your Order
    1. **Secure Packaging:** Use industry-standard materials. Ensure weight/dimensions match your listing.
    2. **Instant Labeling:** Print your pre-paid shipping label from the `Order Details` page.
    3. **Dispatch:** Schedule an in-app courier pickup or visit a designated partner drop-off point.
  </TabsContent>
  <TabsContent label="For Buyers">
    ### Monitoring Arrival
    * **Real-time GPS:** Watch your package move through our network in the `My Purchases` tab.
    * **Automated Alerts:** Receive Push/SMS notifications for "Out for Delivery" and "Delivered" statuses.
  </TabsContent>
</Tabs>

---

## 3. Operating in PAP Mode
For PAP transactions, the seller acts as the logistics coordinator.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Carrier Selection">
    Sellers choose a reputable courier (FedEx, DHL, or specialized freight) and negotiate costs with the buyer via **Artemia Chat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual Tracking Upload">
    Once shipped, the seller **must** manually enter the carrier name and tracking ID into the dashboard to trigger the escrow protection window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct Coordination">
    For high-value or bulky items, buyers and sellers should coordinate delivery windows directly through the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout variant="warning">
  **Insurance Recommendation:** For high-value PAP items, we strongly advise sellers to purchase third-party shipping insurance, as these are not covered by Artemia's internal logistics protection.
</Callout>

---

## 4. Prohibited Shipping Practices
Violations of these policies may result in shipping surcharges, loss of buyer protection, or account suspension.

* **Dimensional Fraud:** Under-declaring weight in PAL mode to reduce fees.
* **Label Circumvention:** Shipping a PAL order using a personal label instead of the generated Artemia label.
* **Hazardous Materials:** Attempting to ship explosives, flammable liquids, or other dangerous goods via standard couriers.

---

## 5. Resolution for Delivery Issues
In the rare event of a logistics failure, follow these escalation paths.

### Damaged Items
* **PAL:** Contact **Artemia Support** immediately. We assume liability for items damaged during our managed transit.
* **PAP:** The buyer and seller should first attempt a resolution via chat. If unsuccessful, escalate to a **Dispute**.

### Missing Packages ("Stolen" or "Lost")
If tracking shows "Delivered" but the item is missing:
1. **Initial Search:** Check with neighbors, building concierge, or "safe drop" locations.
2. **Trace Request:** * **PAL:** We will initiate a formal trace with our courier partners.
    * **PAP:** The seller is responsible for filing a claim with their chosen carrier.

<Callout variant="info">
  **Protection Tip:** Always take a photo of the package at your doorstep or at the drop-off point. Visual evidence is the fastest way to resolve a shipping claim.
</Callout>
