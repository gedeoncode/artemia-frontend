
# Welcome to Artemia Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for the Artemia marketplace. Whether you are here to buy unique items, grow your business as a seller, or understand how our logistics network operates, these guides are designed to help you navigate the platform with ease.

Artemia is a multi-role ecosystem that connects buyers and sellers through two distinct selling modes tailored to different product needs. Use the navigation to find specific guides on account management, listing products, and handling payments.

---

## Understanding User Roles

To get the most out of Artemia, it is important to understand the different ways you can interact with the platform.

### Visitors
Unauthenticated users who can browse listings, search for products, and view public seller profiles.

### Buyers
Registered users who can purchase items, track orders, and communicate with sellers.

### Sellers
Registered users who have activated their shop to list products via PAP or PAL modes.

---

## Selling Modes at a Glance

Artemia offers two ways to sell products. Choosing the right one depends on your inventory and how much of the logistics you want to manage yourself.

### PAP (Post and Promote)
This mode is ideal for high-value or specialized items like real estate, vehicles, and custom services.

- **Payment:** Seller pays a fee at the time of publication.
- **Logistics:** The seller is entirely responsible for shipping, delivery, and providing tracking information to the buyer.

### PAL (Post and Let Artemia)
This is a hands-off fulfillment mode for standard retail goods.

- **Payment:** No upfront cost; Artemia deducts a commission from the final sale.
- **Logistics:** Artemia handles shipping, delivery, and tracking.
- **Payout:** Sellers receive their earnings automatically after delivery and commission deduction.

ℹ️ **Note:** The commission for PAL listings varies based on the product category and price. You will always see the exact breakdown before you click **Publish**.

## Core Pillars of the Platform

### Trust and Safety
We maintain high standards for our marketplace. All users are expected to follow our community guidelines and transaction policies to ensure a fair experience for everyone.

### Payments and Security
Artemia uses secure encryption to process all transactions. Whether you are paying for a PAP listing fee or receiving a payout from a PAL sale, your financial data is protected.

⚠️ **Warning:** Never complete payments for Artemia orders outside of the official platform. Transactions made off-platform are not covered by our buyer protection or dispute resolution services.
